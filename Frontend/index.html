Hand-written algorithms

QuickSort, a min-heap and a hash map implemented without sort(), heapq or Counter, timed live against Python's built-ins on the trip database.

QuickSort

O(n log n) average

Sorts trips by fare, descending. Recurses only into the smaller partition, so the call stack stays O(log n) even with thousands of identical fares.

QuickSort pseudo-code

quicksort(arr, key, lo, hi):
  while lo < hi:
    p = partition(arr, key, lo, hi)
    if p - lo < hi - p:                # recurse into the smaller side
      quicksort(arr, key, lo, p - 1)
      lo = p + 1
    else:
      quicksort(arr, key, p + 1, hi)
      hi = p - 1

partition(arr, key, lo, hi):
  pivot = arr[hi][key]
  i = lo - 1
  for j in lo .. hi - 1:
    if arr[j][key] >= pivot:          # descending order
      i = i + 1
      swap(arr[i], arr[j])
  swap(arr[i + 1], arr[hi])
  return i + 1

Cleaning rules

Outside the reporting month
Pickups before or after the file's dominant month are timestamp errors.
Dropoff at or before pickup, or longer than 24 h
Impossible or stuck-meter durations.
Negative fare
Refunds and meter errors.
Zero distance
Cancelled trips or GPS failures.
Zero passengers
Driver data-entry errors.
Fare over $500 or distance over 100 mi
Outliers that would distort averages.
Fare mismatch
total_amount differs from the sum of its itemized charges by more than $1.
Speed over 100 mph
GPS errors, checked after speed is derived.
Missing passenger count or rate code
Filled with the most common value (1) instead of rejecting the trip.
